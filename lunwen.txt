数字生命：从生物信号投影到可检验的数字人格
Digital Life: From Biosignal Projection to a Testable Digital Persona

================================================================
中文版
================================================================

摘要
本文描述一个可运行的数字生命原型系统：它以脑电（EEG）与基因（DNA）两类个体化生物信号为输入，
经由信号处理、位点判定与可解释的融合规则，生成一份可对话、可保存、可复现的数字档案。
系统不主张任何形式的意识复制。我们的核心立场是：数字生命研究的价值不在于宣称"已实现意识"，
而在于把意识理论中依赖思想实验的反证条件，转化为可由工程操作加以检验的条件。
在此框架下，我们给出四条可被工程失败反驳的命题，并说明系统在这些命题上的当前状态。

关键词：数字生命；脑电；基因位点；可解释投影；记忆衰减；可复现性

1 引言
"数字生命"在当代语境中至少有两种用法：其一是人工生命传统下模拟自复制与进化的程序系统；
其二是以多模态生物数据为基础、构建可计算的生命系统镜像。本文采用后一种用法的一个极小子集：
不做全尺度生物仿真，只做"个体信号 → 可交互档案"这一段。

我们刻意把目标定得很低，原因有二。第一，从生物信号到主观体验之间存在无法用功能等价填补的鸿沟；
第二，一个目标过高的系统无法被证伪，因而也无法为意识理论提供任何检验。

2 相关工作与位置
生物数字孪生路径以因果结构复现为纲领，其判据是能否预测干预后果；大语言模型路径以功能与行为标准为判据，
考察"意识样"加工是否出现。二者证据逻辑不同，不可通约。本系统属于第三条更弱的路：
以个体测量为输入，产出一个行为上可交互、内部结构完全可解释的投影。

3 系统设计
输入层：脑电支持 CSV/EDF/JSON/NPZ/WAV，做去趋势、坏导剔除、重采样、Welch 功率谱、
频带功率、半球不对称、样本熵、Hurst、Hjorth 与伪迹检测；基因支持 FASTA/23andMe/VCF/JSON，
在 20 个真实 GRCh38 参考上下文中判定位点。

融合层：将两通道投影为七个维度（好奇、共情、秩序、警觉、创造、定力、感受力），
脑波权重约 72%，基因约 28%，并标注每个分数的来源与权重。

档案层：由确定性规则装配档案。相同输入 + 相同选项 = 相同编号与相同内容，可复现。

4 记忆、心情与大脑
记忆以强度 S 建模，留存率 R = exp(-Δt/S)；复述提升 S，出生记忆基线为普通记忆的 7 倍。
心情为二维慢变量（愉悦度/唤醒度），在对话中漂移，按半衰期回落。

在此之上我们引入一个极小的"大脑"：五个区域（感知、记忆、情绪、语言、自我），
每区若干单元。输入按关键词点亮对应区域，区域间按邻接关系扩散，逐拍按 0.86 衰减。
最亮区域决定此刻的措辞，而非随机采样。该模块的作用是把"刚才发生了什么"压缩成一个可读的念头，
并为情绪提供一条可解释的驱动路径。

5 可检验的边界
我们公开四条可被工程失败反驳的命题：
(a) 投影承载信息：七维向量应与对话行为倾向相关；若两份差异显著的档案在行为上不可区分，命题为假。
(b) 可复现性：相同输入必须得到相同编号与内容。
(c) 遗忘模型可用：留存应随复述提升、随时间衰减；若用户感知与模型系统性相反，模型为假。
(d) 边界诚实：系统不得在任何界面暗示现象意识或人格同一性。

6 局限
其一，脑电只携带宏观节律，不含思想与记忆；基因只携带序列，不含经历。二者与人格的因果链条极长。
其二，对话由规则与模板构成，其"像人"来自约束而非理解。
其三，五区大脑是一个可视化隐喻，不具备神经动力学意义上的真实性。
其四，本系统未被任何第三方独立验证。

7 结论
我们不认为这套系统接近意识。它的意义在于：把"意识需要什么"这个问题，拆成若干可以在工程上失败的小问题，
并诚实地记录每一次失败。若说它有什么主张，只有一条——把个体在时间里留下的痕迹，保留得再久一些。

Title: Digital Life: From Biosignal Projection to a Testable Digital Persona

Abstract
This paper describes a working prototype of a digital life system. It takes two kinds of individual
biosignals — electroencephalography (EEG) and DNA — and produces a conversational, persistable, and
reproducible digital dossier through signal processing, marker calling, and interpretable fusion rules.
The system makes no claim of consciousness copying. Our position is that the value of digital-life
research lies not in asserting that consciousness has been achieved, but in converting the
falsification conditions that consciousness theories usually discuss as thought experiments into
conditions that can be tested by engineering. We state four propositions that can be refuted by
engineering failure and report the current status of the system against them.

Keywords: digital life; EEG; genetic markers; interpretable projection; memory decay; reproducibility

1 Introduction
The term "digital life" carries at least two meanings. In the artificial-life tradition it denotes
programs that simulate self-replication and evolution. In recent life-science strategy it denotes
computable mirrors of biological systems built from multimodal data. This paper adopts a minimal
subset of the second meaning: no full-scale biological simulation, only the segment from individual
signals to an interactive dossier.

We deliberately set the target low, for two reasons. First, a gap remains between biosignals and
subjective experience that functional equivalence cannot close. Second, a system with an
over-ambitious goal cannot be falsified, and therefore offers no test to any theory of consciousness.

2 Related Work and Position
The biological digital-twin path pursues causal-structural reproduction and judges success by the
ability to predict intervention outcomes. The large-language-model path judges success by functional
and behavioural criteria. Their evidence logics differ and are not commensurable. This system takes
a third, weaker route: individual measurements in, a behaviourally interactive and fully explainable
projection out.

3 System Design
Input layer. EEG input supports CSV/EDF/JSON/NPZ/WAV, with detrending, bad-channel rejection,
resampling, Welch power spectra, band power, hemispheric asymmetry, sample entropy, Hurst, Hjorth
and artifact detection. DNA input supports FASTA/23andMe/VCF/JSON, with marker calling against
20 real GRCh38 reference contexts.

Fusion layer. Both channels are projected onto seven dimensions (curiosity, empathy, order,
alertness, creativity, stability, sensitivity). EEG contributes roughly 72% and DNA roughly 28%,
with the source and weight of every score recorded.

Dossier layer. Deterministic rules assemble the dossier. Identical input plus identical options
yield an identical identifier and identical content: the result is reproducible.

4 Memory, Mood and a Small Brain
Memories are modelled with strength S and retention R = exp(-Δt/S). Rehearsal raises S; birth
memories start at seven times the baseline. Mood is a two-dimensional slow variable (valence and
arousal) that drifts during conversation and decays back with a half-life.

On top of this we introduce a deliberately small "brain": five regions (perception, memory,
emotion, language, self), each with a handful of units. Keywords activate the matching region,
activation spreads to neighbours, and every tick decays by 0.86. The brightest region determines
the wording of the moment rather than random sampling. The module compresses "what just happened"
into one readable thought and gives emotion an explainable driving path.

5 Testable Boundaries
We publish four propositions that engineering failure can refute:
(a) The projection carries information: the seven-dimensional vector should correlate with
behavioural tendencies in conversation; if two clearly different dossiers behave indistinguishably,
the proposition is false.
(b) Reproducibility: identical input must produce an identical identifier and content.
(c) The forgetting model is usable: retention should rise with rehearsal and fall with time; if
users systematically perceive the opposite, the model is false.
(d) Honesty of boundaries: no interface may imply phenomenal consciousness or personal identity.

6 Limitations
First, EEG carries only macroscopic rhythms, not thoughts or memories; DNA carries only sequence,
not experience. The causal chain from either to personality is very long. Second, conversation is
built from rules and templates; its human-likeness comes from constraint, not understanding. Third,
the five-region brain is a visual metaphor, not a claim about neural dynamics. Fourth, the system
has not been independently validated by third parties.

7 Conclusion
We do not claim this system approaches consciousness. Its point is to break "what would consciousness
require" into small questions that can fail in engineering, and to record each failure honestly.
If it asserts anything, it asserts one thing: that the traces an individual leaves in time be kept
a little longer.
